Questions

What counts as one critique?
One photo analysed and scored is one critique. Uploading a new photo uses one; re-reading a critique you've already run doesn't.
Do you store my photos?
No. Your full-resolution photo is sent securely to our AI provider for analysis, then discarded; it is never saved by Second Eye. To make your history meaningful, we keep a small thumbnail (max 256 px, all EXIF and GPS data removed), visible only to you. Delete a critique or your account and the thumbnail is deleted too. Thumbnails are never used for analytics or AI training. Full detail is in our privacy policy.
Can I cancel Pro anytime?
Yes. Cancel whenever you like and you keep Pro until the end of the period you've paid for — no cancellation fee, no questions.
When will Studio be available?
We're opening Studio once enough photographers ask for it. Join the waitlist on the Studio card and you'll be the first to know. Joining is free and doesn't commit you to anything.
What if I reach my monthly limit?
Your allowance renews every month; your account page shows the exact date. Need more before then? Upgrade to Pro for up to 50 critiques a month, or buy a Critique Pack: its credits are used only once your monthly critiques are gone.
Pro or Critique Pack: which one is for me?
If you shoot every week, Pro is far better value: up to 50 critiques a month for 4.90 € (or 49 € a year). If you only want a critique now and then, a pack gives you 12 critiques for 7 €, with no subscription, to use within 12 months (each new pack extends your remaining credits). Pro members can also buy a pack to top up beyond 50.
Can I get a refund?
Your critiques are delivered instantly, so when you buy you're asked to confirm that the service starts straight away, which ends the 14-day withdrawal period. If something goes wrong with a critique, it isn't counted. For any problem, write to hello@getsecondeye.com and we'll sort it out. Full detail in our Terms of Sale.
