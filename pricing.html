Questions

What counts as one critique?
One photo analysed and scored is one critique. Uploading a new photo uses one; re-reading a critique you've already run doesn't.
What happens to my photos?
Your photo is sent for analysis and then discarded — we don't keep uploaded photos on our systems once your critique is generated, and we never use them to train models. Full detail is in our privacy policy.
Can I cancel Pro anytime?
Yes. Cancel whenever you like and you keep Pro until the end of the period you've paid for — no cancellation fee, no questions.
Do pack credits expire?
No. A Critique Pack is a one-time payment and its 10 credits stay on your account until you use them — handy if you only shoot now and then.
What if I reach my monthly limit?
On Free and Pro your critiques reset at the start of each month. Need more before then? Upgrade, or grab a one-off pack to top up without changing your plan.
